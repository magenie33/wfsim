The simulator runs on your machine — start it, then the button above drops you straight into this weapon's build page.

What you can do with it

Build it, fully

8 mod slots + exilus with real drain, polarities and auto-Forma; one arcane; every Incarnon evolution tier. The stats panel explains every number by source.

The real Incarnon cycle

Charge-backed Incarnon form (charges aren't a magazine), transmute in and out, Frenzy on headshots in both forms — simulated as the two-form loop you actually play.

Simulate the fight

Pick an enemy (or build a custom bullet sponge), set headshot rate and duration, configure every buff's stacks and lock — then watch kills, DPS, procs and DoTs come out of a per-shot replay.

Optimize the build

Mark mods, arcanes, evolutions and the exilus slot as fixed or searchable; a Monte-Carlo elimination race across all cores returns a ranked leaderboard, each entry loadable back into the sim.
