# Sobek

Chinese name: 鳄神

Shotgun · Primary · Mastery Rank 7. 70 base damage x5 multishot (impact 52.5, puncture 8.75, slash 8.75), 11% crit chance, 2x crit multiplier, 16.2% status chance.
