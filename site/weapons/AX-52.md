## Not modelled here

- While aiming, headshots get +400% critical chance, additive with Point Strike (wiki). The engine has no headshot-only crit bonus, so the crit chance here is the unaimed one. It is most of the weapon: in a headshot-heavy fight the number reads well low.
- The hip-fire half of the passive, +60% ammo efficiency: the sim aims, and reserves are infinite anyway.
