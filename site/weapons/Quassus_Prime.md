# Quassus Prime

Chinese name: 威震武扇 Prime

Warfan · Melee · Mastery Rank 13. 260 base damage (impact 26, puncture 52, slash 182), 35% crit chance, 2.5x crit multiplier, 18% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- Power Spike's partial combo decay is a Warframe passive and is not modelled: the counter drops to zero when its timer runs out, so with that passive the number reads low.
- Not modelled: First Heavy Attack - Ethereal Daggers and Second Heavy Attack - Ethereal Daggers. Of this weapon's attacks, only the swing, the slam and the heavy slam are.
- The wiki publishes no slide attack figure or duration for this weapon, so there is no slide mode.
- This weapon's heavy attack is a spread of thrown ethereal daggers, 76 base each: 18 at 2.5x, then 9 at 5x, each with a forced Slash status, which is not modelled, so there is no heavy mode and a Tennokai window converts nothing.
- Not modelled: a Warfan's slam hits twice, the heavy slam's second hit at the normal slam's figures.
