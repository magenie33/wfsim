# Ceramic Dagger

Chinese name: 陶瓷匕首

Dagger · Melee · Mastery Rank 3. 140 base damage (impact 14, puncture 126), 10% crit chance, 1.5x crit multiplier, 20% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- Power Spike's partial combo decay is a Warframe passive and is not modelled: the counter drops to zero when its timer runs out, so with that passive the number reads low.
- Not modelled: Spectral Dagger and Spectral Dagger Explosion. Of this weapon's attacks, only the swing, the slam and the heavy slam are.
