# Helminth Claws

Beast Claws · Sentinel · Mastery Rank 0. 200 base damage (slash 200), 7.5% crit chance, 2.5x crit multiplier, 25% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- Attacks are assumed to connect. Companion movement, contact geometry, attack selection, precepts and posture target selection are not simulated.
