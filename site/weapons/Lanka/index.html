Support WFSim

WFSim is free software, licensed AGPL-3.0. Building, simulating and optimizing are the whole of this site, and they are the same for everyone: no account, no payment, no part held back.

The engine, the game data and the wasm it all runs on are public. If you ever stop recognising what this becomes, you can run your own — that is not my assurance, it is the licence.

It does take money and time to run. If it has ever saved you a Forma, you can chip in.

Why you can check it

  • Every formula in the engine cites its source — a wiki page, a datamine, or a measurement — and every golden test is calibrated against a real in-game run.
  • It tells you what it cannot model, on the card where you are standing: on a weapon, an evolution, a mod, an arcane, an enemy. It lists those itself rather than waiting to be asked.
  • Sometimes it says the number is right and the game is wrong, and names the bug a hotfix would take away.
  • And if your own in-game measurement disagrees with it, the measurement wins. That is a bug report about reality, and it is the one thing that changes what this engine does.

Where it goes

  • The wfsim.app domain, and its renewal.
  • Cloudflare — the bandwidth and build minutes that serve the app and its art from one origin.
  • Time: new weapons, new mechanics, and the in-game measurements every golden test is calibrated against.
