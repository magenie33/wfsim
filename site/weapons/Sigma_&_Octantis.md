# Sigma & Octantis

Chinese name: 西格玛 & 南极座

Sword And Shield · Melee · Mastery Rank 10. 174 base damage (impact 38.28, puncture 27.84, slash 107.88), 28% crit chance, 2.2x crit multiplier, 16% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- Power Spike's partial combo decay is a Warframe passive and is not modelled: the counter drops to zero when its timer runs out, so with that passive the number reads low.
- Not modelled: Shield Throw and Shield Throw Explosion. Of this weapon's attacks, only the swing, the slam and the heavy slam are.
- The wiki gives this weapon combos of its own (aerial attack), which replace the stance's and are not transcribed; the stance's (or its class's) are used instead.
- The wiki publishes no slide attack figure or duration for this weapon, so there is no slide mode.
- Not modelled: each blocked attack stores a charge of +15% Critical Chance and +15% Status Chance for the next attack, up to 4.
