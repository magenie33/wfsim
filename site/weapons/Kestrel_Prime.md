# Kestrel Prime

Chinese name: 红隼 Prime

Glaive · Melee · Mastery Rank 11. 210 base damage (impact 157.5, puncture 21, slash 31.5), 20% crit chance, 2.2x crit multiplier, 40% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- Power Spike's partial combo decay is a Warframe passive and is not modelled: the counter drops to zero when its timer runs out, so with that passive the number reads low.
- A glaive's heavy attack is the detonation of a thrown glaive, which is its own mode (a throw, detonated), so there is no standing heavy mode and a Tennokai window converts no swing.
