# Steflos Prime

Chinese name: 石晶之花 Prime

Shotgun · Primary · Mastery Rank 15. 480 base damage (heat 285, impact 195), 18% crit chance, 2.2x crit multiplier, 28% status chance.

Current published rankings and revisions: https://wfsim.app/api/board/pages/catalog?weapon=steflos_prime

## Not modelled here

- Projectile lifetime and growth are not modelled: the base lifetime is 0.6 s, each enemy hit adds 50% lifetime up to 10 s, and the projectile grows in size. The simulation uses a fixed 2 m width and does not stop the projectile when its lifetime expires.
