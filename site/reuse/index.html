Using and citing WFSim

Guides, videos and streams

You may quote individual WFSim measurements and benchmark results, and reproduce short excerpts from our measurement reports, to explain a build or make a comparison in your own work. WFSim grants this permission worldwide, royalty-free, including for work that earns advertising or sponsorship revenue. You can publish under this permission without contacting us.

Credit WFSim and link to the source page or shared build. For a contributed measurement, also credit the contributor named in the entry. For a score, identify the benchmark or fight settings so readers can reproduce the comparison. In a video or stream, the source link can go in the description or show notes.

For example, when comparing two Braton builds, show the scores alongside the benchmark you ran and credit WFSim’s Braton page. Include the build links if you want viewers to try the comparison themselves.

Search and AI answers

Search engines may index WFSim’s public pages. AI assistants may retrieve them to answer a user’s question. Link to the original result so the reader can check the build and its assumptions. The machine-readable index links to weapon pages that can be read without starting the simulator. Cache published files across lookups and respect the service’s rate limits.

WFSim reserves the use of its protected content for training or fine-tuning AI models, including text and data mining for those purposes, wherever applicable law permits that reservation. The crawler directives express those preferences for automated clients.

For a commercial data feed, a regularly synchronised copy of the leaderboard, or integration of the calibration collection into a commercial product, contact WFSim about licensing if the use falls outside an existing licence or legal exception.

The engine is available under AGPL-3.0-or-later, including for commercial use. If you run a modified version as a network service, offer its corresponding source to users who interact with it remotely, as required by the licence. Contact the same address for a proprietary integration that needs different terms. The brand policy allows you to credit WFSim and describe compatibility. Using the name or logo as your product’s identity or claiming official endorsement requires permission.

Project partnerships

WFSim welcomes proposals for community challenges, creator collaborations, commissioned build comparisons and custom integrations. Email WFSim with the question you want to answer or the event you are planning. We agree on the scope, fee and credits for each project.

A sponsor can fund the work, choose the research question and receive credit. Published scores follow the same benchmark rules for everyone. Sponsored projects use the same public compute queue rules as other requests. Identify the project sponsor in your coverage. Uses of Warframe assets are subject to Digital Extremes’ content policy.
