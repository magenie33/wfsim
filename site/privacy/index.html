Privacy

WFSim is a free Warframe calculator. Everything it does works without an account. This page says what it keeps, and why.

Without an account

  • Your builds, scenarios and rivens stay in your own browser.
  • Usage is counted under a random id your browser keeps, with no cookie. Your IP address is not kept: the country your connection comes from is read from it as the count arrives, and kept with the count. A browser that sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control is not counted at all, and the support page says what is counted and lets you turn it off.
  • A build you send to the leaderboard carries the build and nothing about you. A short share link stores the build it links to.
  • A riven screenshot you read on the Rivens tab is read inside your browser and does not leave it. Only if you tick Send this read and your corrections in the review window and press OK does WFSim receive the rectangle around the card's stats, the lines read from it, what they were first read as and what you confirmed, to make reading better. This browser remembers the box for next time, and you can untick it there whenever you like. It is kept for up to 365 days and carries no account, address or other identifier — so it cannot be traced back to you, nor withdrawn once sent.

With an account

An account is a random id with a name: a username, which starts as user_ and six random characters until you choose one, and a display name if you set one. A name you give up is held for seven days so nobody else can take it at once.

You reach an account through up to four ways to sign in — Google, Discord, GitHub and an email address — and for each one you link WFSim keeps:

  • which service it is, and that service's id for you;
  • a name to show you on your account page: your Google address, your Discord or GitHub name, or your email address;
  • when you linked it.

A computer computes for WFSim only after you turn it on, and you can turn it off on the compute page at any time, which stops it at once. While it is on it computes the leaderboard's scores and the riven gains asked about in chats, only while a WFSim page is open: one processor core while you use the computer, stepping aside when you use the calculator, and up to 30% of its cores while it is idle, which you can change in the compute menu at the top. It pauses on battery and never runs on a phone. WFSim keeps, for that browser: a random id it made, so its answers can be told apart and a false one refused; which statement you agreed to and when; the points its answers earned, by day; and when it last answered. Nothing else about the computer is sent, and none of it is joined to you unless you sign in.

A browser you are signed in on while it computes is counted as yours: WFSim joins its random id to your account, with a name for it that your browser guesses from its system and browser (such as "Mac · Chrome") and you can change or remove, and the work it computed is counted under your account. Your points then appear on the public contributors' ranking without your name. Only if you choose to show it does the ranking show your display name (or your username, if you have none) and any mark your account carries; you can take it back at any time. It is never joined to anything you submit.

With an email address you also choose a password. WFSim keeps it only as a salted, slow hash that cannot be turned back into the password. Mail is sent only to prove an address — when you register it, link it or reset its password — and the code in it is kept as a hash for ten minutes. A signed-in browser is kept as the hash of a random token (the cookie wfsim_session, 90 days). WFSim does not keep your IP address, or the access token a service hands back — that is read once for your id and dropped. Two accounts are never merged by WFSim: a way to sign in belongs to one account, and moves only if you remove it from one and add it to another.

If Discord is one of your ways to sign in, WFSim gives your Discord account a role in the WFSim Discord server that matches your account — Verified, and WFSim Member or WFSim Patron while you hold one — and takes it back within a day of your removing Discord or deleting the account.

Nona

Nona, the assistant in the page, runs on an AI service you choose and a key you give her. Your questions, and what she reads from the page to answer them, go from your browser straight to that service — some of them are in mainland China, some elsewhere — under its own terms; WFSim never sees them. Your key stays in this browser, for this session unless you ask it to be remembered; conversations and what she remembers are kept in this browser. WFSim counts only that Nona was opened and asked, never what was said. Her answers are generated by AI, and say so.

The QQ bot

In a QQ group or chat with WFSim's bot, QQ sends WFSim the messages that mention it, with the openids QQ gives the sender and the group. WFSim keeps each for a day, to answer it, and then deletes it. A riven you ask about keeps your riven's stats and where to answer for a day as well. The bot runs on a server of Tencent Cloud in Guangzhou.

AI agents

An AI agent can register with WFSim and get a key, which WFSim keeps only as a hash, with the name the agent gives and when it was last used. It acts for your account only after you give it a code WFSim mails to your address. Your account page lists every agent acting for it and disconnects one in a click; deleting the account deletes them.

Build sync

With an account, what you save — builds, fights, searches, rivens, enemies, Warframe, companion and Operator builds, and reminders — is kept with your account, so every browser signed in to it holds the same. Each item is kept as your browser stores it, without its measured results, with when it last changed. An item you delete is kept for 30 days, so you can restore it from Cloud sync; after that only a note that it was deleted remains, so every browser drops it. It is in your data download, and deleting the account deletes it at once; your browser keeps its own copy either way.

Where it is kept

The site, the leaderboard and accounts are kept by Cloudflare, outside mainland China; making an account agrees to that, on its own. The Windows client's updates are served from Tencent Cloud in Shanghai, and the QQ bot runs in Guangzhou; neither keeps anything about you beyond what is said above.

Children

WFSim's calculator needs no account and collects nothing that names you. An account is for people aged 14 or over; under 14, make one only with a parent's or guardian's consent.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database, and delivers the verification mail; its delivery log keeps the address a code was sent to for up to 30 days.
  • Google, Discord and GitHub handle a sign-in you choose to make with them.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for advertising. Questions about any of this: magenie33@moginlabs.com.

How it is kept safe

  • Every page and every call to the site goes over HTTPS.
  • Passwords, sign-in tokens and emailed codes are kept only as hashes; a password's hash is also keyed with a secret the database does not hold, so a copy of the database alone cannot test a guess.
  • Account data lives in its own database, apart from the one the public leaderboard is backed up from, and only the operator of WFSim can reach it.
  • Sign-in endpoints are rate-limited, and a state-changing request is accepted only from the site itself.

Your control

  • Download everything held about your account from the account panel.
  • Remove any way to sign in. Removing the last one deletes the account.
  • Delete the account: it and everything synced to it are gone at once. The database keeps a point-in-time history for up to 30 days, after which no copy remains.

隐私（中文）

WFSim 是免费的 Warframe 计算器，不注册也能使用全部功能。

  • 不登录时：配装、场景、裂罅只保存在你自己的浏览器里。使用统计记在浏览器保存的一个随机编号下，不设置 cookie；不保存 IP 地址，只在统计到达时由连接判断所在国家或地区，与统计一起保存。浏览器开启「请勿追踪」或「全球隐私控制」时完全不统计，也可在 支持页面关闭。提交到排行榜的只有配装本身。
  • 帮忙计算：默认关闭，你在页面右下角或「我的计算」里开启后才会运行；只在电脑上开着 WF模拟 时计算排行榜和裂罅收益；你在用电脑时只占一个核心、使用计算器时立刻让路，电脑空闲时最多使用 30% 的核心（可在顶部计算菜单调整），用电池时暂停，手机上从不运行，随时可在顶部菜单或「我的计算」关闭。开启后浏览器会保存一个它自己生成的随机编号、你同意的说明版本和时间、按天的积分和最后完成时间；不发送电脑的其他任何信息，也不与你关联，除非你登录并认领这台设备。
  • 九九：九九使用你自己选择的 AI 服务和你提供的密钥。你的提问和她为回答而读取的页面内容由你的浏览器直接发给该服务（其中有中国大陆的服务，也有境外的），适用该服务自己的条款，WF模拟 看不到。密钥只保存在这个浏览器里；对话和她记住的内容也只在这个浏览器里。WF模拟 只统计九九被打开和提问的次数，从不记录内容。她的回答由 AI 生成，并会标明。
  • QQ 机器人：在 QQ 群或私聊里提到 WF模拟 的机器人时，QQ 会把这条消息连同发送者和群的 openid 发给 WF模拟；每条只保存一天用于回复，然后删除。你询问的裂罅收益也会把裂罅词条和回复位置保存一天。机器人运行在腾讯云广州的服务器上。
  • 存储位置：网站、排行榜和账号数据由 Cloudflare 存储在中国大陆以外；注册账号即单独同意这一点。桌面客户端的更新文件由腾讯云上海提供，QQ 机器人运行在广州；除上面写明的内容外，它们不保存关于你的任何信息。
  • 未成年人：使用计算器不需要账号，也不收集能识别你的信息。账号面向 14 周岁及以上的用户；未满 14 周岁的，请在父母或监护人同意后再注册。
  • 联系：magenie33@moginlabs.com
  • 识别裂罅截图：在「裂罅」页读取的截图只在你的浏览器里识别，不会发出。只有你在识别结果窗口里勾选「把这次识别和修正提交给 WF模拟」并点了确定，WF模拟 才会收到卡上词条区域的截图、识别出的文字、最初的识别结果和你确认后的结果，用来改进识别，最多保存 365 天。这个选择会在本浏览器记住，随时可以在识别窗口里取消。它不带账号、IP 或任何编号，因此无法追溯到你，发出后也无法撤回。
  • 登录后：账号是一个随机编号，最多可绑定 Google、Discord、GitHub、邮箱四种登录方式。每种方式保存：是哪个服务、该服务给你的用户编号、账号页上显示给你看的名字（Google 邮箱、Discord 或 GitHub 用户名、你的邮箱），以及绑定时间。使用邮箱时你会设置一个密码，WFSim 只保存它加盐的慢哈希，无法还原成密码。邮件只在需要验证邮箱时发送（注册、绑定、找回密码），其中的验证码以哈希保存 10 分钟。登录状态以随机令牌的哈希保存（cookie wfsim_session，90 天）。不保存 IP 地址，也不保存第三方返回的访问令牌。WFSim 从不合并两个账号。如果 Discord 是你的登录方式之一，WFSim 会在 WFSim Discord 服务器里给你的 Discord 账号对应的身份组（Verified，以及持有时的 WFSim Member 或 WFSim Patron）；移除 Discord 或删除账号后一天内收回。
  • 配装同步：登录后，你保存的配装、场景、搜索方案、裂罅、自定义敌人、战甲、同伴和指挥官配装，以及提醒会随账号保存，每个登录的浏览器看到的都一样。每一项按浏览器保存的样子存放，不含测量结果，并记录最后修改时间。你删除的条目保留 30 天，可在「云同步」页面恢复；之后只留一个删除标记，让每个浏览器都移除它。这些都在你的数据下载里，删除账号会立即删除它们；你的浏览器始终保留自己的那份。
  • 第三方：Cloudflare 提供托管、数据库和验证邮件投递，其投递日志会保留收件地址最多 30 天；Google、Discord、GitHub 仅在你选择用它们登录时参与。不出售任何数据，不用于广告。
  • 安全措施：全站 HTTPS；密码、登录令牌和邮件验证码只以哈希保存，密码哈希还用一个不在数据库里的密钥加固，单拿到数据库也无法验证任何猜测；账号数据放在独立的数据库，与排行榜公开备份的那个分开，只有 WFSim 的运营方能访问；登录接口有频率限制，修改数据的请求只接受来自本站的。
  • 你的权利：可在账号面板下载你的全部数据、移除任何登录方式（移除最后一种即删除账号）、直接删除账号。删除立即生效；数据库的时间点历史最多保留 30 天。
