# WFSim trademark policy

**"WFSim", the WFSim wordmark and the WFSim logo are unregistered trademarks of
the project, and they are not covered by [AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE).** The
license grants rights in the code. It grants nothing in the name.

One thing is being protected: that when a reader sees the name WFSim, they know
whose numbers they are looking at. A calculator's whole value is that its
figures can be trusted and checked, so a name on the wrong figures costs more
here than branding usually does.

## What you may do, without asking

Referential use — naming the project to talk about it — is always fine:

- **Compatibility.** "Compatible with WFSim", "reads WFSim share links",
  "imports builds from WFSim".
- **Origin.** "Based on WFSim", "a fork of WFSim", "powered by the WFSim
  engine".
- **Discussion.** Mentioning, reviewing, comparing, criticising or linking the
  project in articles, videos, posts and documentation, with the logo where it
  identifies the project being discussed.

Keeping the license headers and copyright notices that source files carry is
required by the license, and this policy does not touch it.

## What needs permission

- Naming or branding a product, service, site or application **WFSim**, or any
  name close enough to be confused with it.
- The wordmark or logo as the icon, brand or identity of something that is not
  this project.
- Anything that implies official status, endorsement, affiliation or
  maintenance by this project.
- Distributing a **modified version under the WFSim name or logo**. The license
  gives you the right to modify and redistribute the code; this is the one
  thing it asks you to rename.

## Forks and derived projects

Fork freely — the license invites it. Give the result **your own name**, your
own icon and your own domain, then say what it descends from in plain prose:
"based on WFSim" is accurate, welcome, and needs no permission.

The reason is practical rather than proprietary. Two calculators under one name
produce two answers to one question, and no reader can tell which is which or
which to report a discrepancy against.
